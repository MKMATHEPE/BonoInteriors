Advertising Guidelines

At Infinity, we are committed to maintaining the integrity, professionalism, and legal compliance of all billboard and mural advertising campaigns. These guidelines outline what is permitted and expected when submitting content for display.

1. Content Standards

  • All advertisements must be accurate, clear, and not misleading.
  • Claims must be substantiated and not exaggerated.
  • Advertisements must comply with the Advertising Standards Authority of South Africa (ASA) Code of Conduct.

2. Prohibited Content

  • No content containing hate speech, discrimination, or defamatory language.
  • No content promoting illegal activities, violence, or harmful behavior.
  • No sexually explicit, vulgar, or offensive visuals or language.
  • No political or religious content unless approved in writing.

3. Technical Specifications

  • Files should be high-resolution (minimum 300 DPI) and in PNG, JPEG, or PDF format.
  • Billboard Dimensions: Small (3m x 6m), Medium (6m x 12m), Large (12m x 24m).
  • Mural Dimensions: 2.5m x 5m (standard).
  • Ensure safe zones and bleed margins are respected.

4. Approval Process

  • All artwork will be reviewed before printing or display.
  • Infinity reserves the right to reject or request edits to any design that does not meet these guidelines.
  • Clients will be notified of approval status within 24–48 hours of submission.

5. Installation Guidelines

  • Approved designs will be scheduled for installation based on availability.
  • Installation dates may be affected by weather or site conditions.
  • Clients will be notified once their advertisement is live.

6. Duration & Updates

  • Campaign durations range from 1 week to 12 months, as agreed in the contract.
  • Clients may update content mid-campaign, but additional design or installation fees may apply.

7. Contact for Questions

For more information or clarification, please reach out to our support team.
