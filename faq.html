Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What services does Infinity offer?

Infinity specializes in mural and billboard advertising, providing businesses with high-visibility marketing solutions in strategic locations.

2. Where are your advertising spaces located?

Our advertising spaces are located on Old Johannesburg Road at the Lifestyle Centre intersection and other high-traffic areas in South Africa.

3. How much does it cost to advertise with Infinity?

Pricing varies based on location, duration, and size of the advertisement. Contact us for a customized quote tailored to your business needs.

4. How long can my advertisement stay up?

We offer flexible advertising durations, ranging from short-term campaigns to long-term placements. Clients can choose from monthly, quarterly, or annual advertising contracts.

5. What are the requirements for submitting artwork?

We accept high-resolution digital files in formats such as PNG, JPEG, or PDF. If you need design assistance, our team can help create professional advertising visuals.

6. Can I change my advertisement after it has been placed?

Yes, you can update your advertisement, but additional costs may apply depending on production and installation requirements.

7. Are the advertising spaces illuminated?

Yes, our mural advertising spaces feature solar-powered lighting to ensure 24/7 visibility.

8. Do you provide discounts for long-term advertising?

Yes, we offer special discounts for extended advertising contracts. Contact us to discuss available packages and rates.

9. How can I book an advertising space?

You can book by contacting us through email, phone, or via our website. Our team will guide you through the process and help you choose the best advertising option for your brand.
