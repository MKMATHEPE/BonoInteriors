Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What services does Bono Interior Design Studio offer?

We offer full-service interior design, including concept development, space planning, material and furniture selection, styling, and final installation. We also assist with renovations, refresh projects, and styling for completed spaces.

2. Do you work on residential or commercial projects?

Our primary focus is residential interiors, including apartments, homes, and private spaces. We selectively take on small commercial projects that align with our design aesthetic and values.

3. What is your design style?

Our style is refined, modern, and timeless, with an emphasis on functionality and warmth. While we have a signature look, every project is tailored to the client’s lifestyle, taste, and needs.

4. How does the design process work?

Our process typically includes an initial consultation, concept and mood board development, space planning and design detailing, selection of finishes, furniture, and décor, followed by styling and project completion. We guide you through every step to ensure a seamless and enjoyable experience.

5. Do I need a big budget to work with you?

No. We work with a range of budgets and are transparent about costs from the start. Our goal is to maximize value and deliver the best possible result within your budget.

6. Can you work with existing furniture or décor?

Absolutely. We love incorporating meaningful or existing pieces and blending them thoughtfully with new elements to create a cohesive and refreshed space.

7. How long does an interior design project take?

Timelines vary depending on the scope of the project. A styling or refresh project may take a few weeks, while full design or renovation projects can take several months. We provide estimated timelines upfront.

8. Do you offer online or virtual design services?

Yes, we offer virtual design services for clients who are not based locally or prefer a remote process.

9. When should I contact an interior designer?

The earlier, the better — especially for renovations or new builds. Involving us early helps avoid costly mistakes and ensures a cohesive design from the start.

10. How do I get started?

You can get started by contacting us through our website or email to book an initial consultation. We’ll discuss your vision, needs, timeline, and next steps.
