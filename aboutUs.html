About Us

Infinity is a family-owned mural and billboard advertising company strategically located on Old Johannesburg Road at the intersection of the Lifestyle Centre in South Africa.

Our mission is to provide exceptional, reliable, and customer-friendly advertising solutions that help businesses and brands gain maximum exposure.

Our Mission

To revolutionize out-of-home advertising by delivering high-impact, strategically placed murals and billboards that enhance brand visibility while maintaining affordability and sustainability.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of innovative and eco-friendly advertising solutions in South Africa, setting new standards in quality and customer satisfaction.

What We Offer

  • Mural Advertising: Our high-quality murals measure 2.5m x 5m and are illuminated with solar-powered lights to ensure visibility day and night.
  • Billboard Advertising: Premium billboard spaces at high-traffic locations, offering maximum brand exposure.
  • Customized Advertising Solutions: Tailored campaigns to suit various business needs, ensuring effective audience engagement.

Why Choose Infinity?

  • Strategic Location: Prime advertising spots with high foot and vehicle traffic.
  • Eco-Friendly Solutions: Solar-powered lighting for sustainable advertising.
  • Family-Owned Values: A commitment to reliability, excellence, and personalized customer service.
  • Affordable Pricing: Competitive rates that ensure value for your investment.

Our Commitment

At Infinity, we believe in building long-term relationships with our clients by offering hassle-free and result-driven advertising solutions. Our team is dedicated to providing creative, impactful, and memorable outdoor advertising services.
